Privacy notice

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Steady Mind 35 ("we", "us") provides general wellness information for men over 35 through social media and email. We are based in England, UK.

Contact: hello@steadymind35.com.

What we collect

When you sign up for the free 7-Day Reset or other emails, we collect:

  • your email address (required)
  • your first name (optional, if you choose to give it)

We do not ask for health details, symptoms, medical history or other special category data. Please do not send those by email. For health questions, speak to your GP or another qualified professional.

Why we use your data

We use your email (and optional first name) only to:

  • send the free 7-Day Reset you asked for
  • send occasional Steady Mind 35 emails with wellness tips and updates, when you have consented

The legal basis is your consent. You can withdraw it at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any email, or by emailing us.

Who processes your data

We use MailerLite as our email and form provider. MailerLite processes subscriber data on our behalf. MailerLite stores data in the EU. See MailerLite's own privacy information on their website.

We do not sell your email address. We do not share it with other companies for their marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while you remain subscribed. If you unsubscribe, we stop marketing emails. MailerLite may keep a suppressed address so we do not email you again by mistake. You can ask us to delete your data (see Your rights).

Your rights (UK GDPR)

You can ask us to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you
  • correct inaccurate data
  • erase your data
  • restrict or object to certain processing
  • receive a copy of your data in a portable form (where that right applies)

To make a request, email hello@steadymind35.com. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): https://ico.org.uk/

Cookies and the website

This website doesn't use analytics or advertising cookies. When you submit the sign-up form, your details are sent to MailerLite; the form doesn't set cookies on this site.

Children

Our content and emails are aimed at adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this notice. The date at the top of the page will change when we do. Important changes will be reflected here before they apply to new sign-ups.
