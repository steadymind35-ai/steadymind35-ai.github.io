Privacy notice

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Steady Mind 35 ("we", "us") provides general wellness information for men over 35 through social media and email. We are based in England, UK.

Contact: hello@steadymind35.com.

What we collect

When you sign up for the free 7-Day Reset or other emails, we collect:

  • your email address (required)
  • your first name (optional, if you choose to give it)

When you sign up from one of our videos, we may also record which platform you came from (for example YouTube, Instagram or TikTok), taken from the link you clicked. This is stored with your sign-up in MailerLite.

We do not ask for health details, symptoms, medical history or other special category data. Please do not send those by email. For health questions, speak to your GP or another qualified professional.

Why we use your data

We use your email (and optional first name) only to:

  • send the free 7-Day Reset you asked for
  • send occasional Steady Mind 35 emails with wellness tips and updates, when you have consented

We use the platform you came from to:

  • understand which of our channels people used to find the 7-Day Reset, so we can improve the site and how we share it

For your email address and first name, the legal basis is your consent. You can withdraw it at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any email, or by emailing us. For the platform you came from, the legal basis is our legitimate interests (running and improving the service).

Who processes your data

We use MailerLite as our email and form provider. MailerLite processes subscriber data on our behalf. MailerLite stores data in the EU. See MailerLite's own privacy information on their website.

We use GoatCounter to count visits to this website in aggregate. GoatCounter is operated from Ireland and stores data in the EU (Finland and Germany). See GoatCounter's own privacy information on their website.

We do not sell your email address. We do not share it with other companies for their marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while you remain subscribed. If you unsubscribe, we stop marketing emails. MailerLite may keep a suppressed address so we do not email you again by mistake. You can ask us to delete your data (see Your rights).

Your rights (UK GDPR)

You can ask us to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you
  • correct inaccurate data
  • erase your data
  • restrict or object to certain processing
  • receive a copy of your data in a portable form (where that right applies)

To make a request, email hello@steadymind35.com. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): https://ico.org.uk/

Cookies and the website

This website does not use cookies or similar storage on your device for analytics or advertising, and we do not show a cookie banner.

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly visit counter, to see how many people view our pages and which of our links they used (for example YouTube, Instagram or TikTok). GoatCounter does not set cookies and does not store IP addresses or other identifiers that would let us recognise you. It keeps only aggregate figures, such as daily page views and campaign totals.

The sign-up form sends your details to MailerLite and does not set cookies on this site.

If you have questions about this, email hello@steadymind35.com.

Children

Our content and emails are aimed at adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this notice. The date at the top of the page will change when we do. Important changes will be reflected here before they apply to new sign-ups.
